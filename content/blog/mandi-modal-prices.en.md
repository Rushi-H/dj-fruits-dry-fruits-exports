## Demystifying Mandi Daily Price Sheets

Every morning, Agricultural Produce Market Committees (APMC) across Maharashtra publish three distinct price figures: Minimum, Maximum, and Modal.

### What is the Modal Price?
- **Minimum Price**: The lowest price accepted, usually for damaged, rain-affected, or undersized culls.
- **Maximum Price**: The absolute peak bid for an exceptional, boutique auction lot (often representing less than 5% of total arrivals).
- **Modal Price**: The price at which the **largest physical volume** of produce was traded on that auction floor.

### Why Modal Price Matters for Farm-Gate Negotiation
When an exporter or wholesale aggregator offers to buy directly from your orchard, comparing their offer against the **Modal Price** gives an honest benchmark. If your crop meets export grade (size, brix, residue limits), you should command a premium over the domestic modal benchmark.
