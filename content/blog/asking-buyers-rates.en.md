## Safeguarding Your Produce Before Signing a Deal

Entering into a procurement agreement without ironclad terms is the single largest risk grape and pomegranate growers face during peak season.

### 1. "What is your exact deduction policy for culls?"
Never accept ambiguous phrases like "standard mandi deduction." Clarify:
- What percentage of sorting loss is absorbed by the buyer?
- Are sub-grade berries returned to you, or purchased at a secondary processing rate?

### 2. "Where and how is the produce weighed?"
Ensure all weighing is conducted on **calibrated electronic platform scales** with a printed or digital slip issued on the spot.

### 3. "What is the confirmed payment settlement timeline?"
A credible exporter should settle via RTGS / NEFT transfer directly to your bank account within 24 to 48 hours of lot grading and receipt verification.

### 4. "Who bears transport and crate loss costs?"
Confirm whether farm gate pickup includes crate loading, transit insurance, and unloading labor at the packhouse.
